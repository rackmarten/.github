# Privacy policy

This covers the small tool rackmarten uses with its own Instagram account
([@rackmarten](https://www.instagram.com/rackmarten)), registered with Meta
as "rackmarten comments".

## What it does

It reads the comments on rackmarten's own Instagram posts and the posts'
public numbers (views, likes, shares), so I can see what people think of the
mods and answer them. It only ever connects to the rackmarten account. It
doesn't post, reply, like or message anyone; I answer comments myself.

## What it keeps

The text of each comment, the commenter's username, when it was written and
a link to it, plus the daily numbers per post. They're stored and processed
only on private servers I run myself, and used only to read and answer
feedback about the rackmarten projects.

Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, used for ads or used to build
profiles of people.

## Removing your data

Delete your comment on Instagram and it won't be collected again. To have a
comment that was already stored removed, send a message to
[@rackmarten](https://www.instagram.com/rackmarten) or ask on the
[rackmarten Discord](https://discord.gg/C687NV4dCp) and I'll delete it.
